Established in Doha by Mohammed Al Mohannadi, Studio Ajid approaches each project as a story shaped around individual needs, identity, and ways of living.

Culture is seen not as a fixed aesthetic, but as a living source of inspiration. Reinterpreted through a contemporary lens, it informs every scale of the studio’s work, from spaces to objects, creating designs that feel rooted in their context without being bound by tradition.
